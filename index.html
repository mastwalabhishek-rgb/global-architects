About the Firm

Designing India's
Most Thoughtful Spaces

Founded in 2017, Global Architects is a Dehradun-based practice focused on creating architecture that is deeply connected to its landscape. We believe great architecture begins with a deep understanding of people, place, and purpose.

Rooted in the foothills of the Himalayas, we bring the discipline of mountain-scale thinking to every project — from intimate residences to landmark commercial spaces across India.

  • Residential Architecture
  • Commercial Design
  • Interior Planning
  • Landscape Integration

How We Work

From First Conversation
to Final Stone

  1. 01

    Listen

    Every project begins with conversation, not sketches. We spend time understanding your life, your aspirations, and the way you want to inhabit space.

  2. 02

    Imagine

    Concepts drawn from place, material, and light. Every sketch is a response to your land, your climate, and your story — never a template applied.

    Build

    Precision from blueprint to final stone. We remain on-site, hands-on, and accountable through every phase of construction.
