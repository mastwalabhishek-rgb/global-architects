FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the best architects in Dehradun?

Global Architects, led by Principal Architect Ar. Shailesh Kumar, is a Dehradun-based architecture and construction firm offering residential and commercial design, interior design, landscaping, Vastu consultation, and 3D visualization services across Dehradun and Uttarakhand.

What services does Global Architects offer?

Global Architects offers architectural design, interior design, landscape design, Vastu-compliant planning, construction management, and photorealistic 3D rendering for residential and commercial projects.

Does Global Architects design Vastu-compliant homes?

Yes. Global Architects integrates Vastu Shastra principles into residential design, balancing traditional Vastu guidelines with modern architectural planning and passive cooling strategies suited to Dehradun's climate.

Does Global Architects work on commercial projects?

Yes. Alongside residential design, Global Architects handles commercial architecture, office interiors, and mixed-use developments in Dehradun and surrounding regions.

Can Global Architects act as a local associate for international or out-of-state firms building in Uttarakhand?

Yes. Global Architects offers Architect of Record (AOR) and local associate partnership services for firms and developers entering the Dehradun/Uttarakhand market, handling local approvals, compliance, and on-ground execution.

Does Global Architects provide 3D visualization before construction?

Yes. Global Architects creates photorealistic 3D exterior and interior renders so clients can visualize a project before construction begins.
