Areas We Serve

Local architecture coverage across Uttarkashi district

Uttarkashi

Uttarkashi city is our core district lead, where we plan homes for steep sites, river-edge houses and guest lodges that respond to both weather and local craft.

Purola

In Purola, our work focuses on life near the Yamuna and mountain roads, balancing tourism-ready design with strong foundations for hillside construction.

Barkot

Barkot's busy pilgrimage route needs durable architecture. We design homes, guest houses and shops that respect the hill slopes and local weather cycles.

Chinyalisaur

Chinyalisaur sits at the edge of the hills and plains. Our design practice here combines modern shelter with traditional form, especially for family homes and small resorts.

Naugaon

Naugaon's river valleys call for grounded, comfortable homes. We guide clients through construction, planning and energy-smart design for this scenic location.
