Interior Design Cost in Dehradun 2026 — Complete Per Sq Ft Guide

June 2026 / 6 min read / Global Architects

Once the walls are up and the structure is complete, the next question is almost always the same — how much will the interiors cost?

It is a question worth taking seriously, because interior design in Dehradun is not a fixed number. The same 1,500 sq ft home can cost ₹8 lakh to finish or ₹40 lakh — depending on materials, scope, and the quality of execution. This guide breaks down what interior design actually costs in Dehradun in 2026, what drives the price up or down, and what to watch out for when comparing quotes.

Interior Design Cost in Dehradun — 2026 Rate Overview

Based on current Dehradun market rates, interior design costs typically fall in the following ranges:

Basic Interiors — ₹400 to ₹700 per sq ft

Standard materials, basic modular furniture, simple false ceiling, regular flooring. Suitable for rental properties, budget homes, or projects where functionality matters more than finish. Most local contractors in Dehradun operate in this range.

Mid-Range Interiors — ₹700 to ₹1,200 per sq ft

Better material grade — BWR plywood, laminates, semi-premium flooring, designer lighting. This is the most commonly chosen range for new residential homes in Dehradun — covers areas like GMS Road, Patel Nagar, Haridwar Road, and Sahastradhara Road. Covers full home interiors including kitchen, wardrobes, living room, and bedrooms with reasonable quality across all rooms.

Premium Interiors — ₹1,200 to ₹2,000 per sq ft

High-quality materials — marine plywood, veneer finish, imported tiles, premium sanitary fittings, custom furniture, detailed lighting design. Typical for homes on Rajpur Road, Mussoorie Road, Race Course, and Vasant Vihar. Projects where the owner wants a finished result that genuinely reflects their personality and lifestyle.

Luxury Interiors — ₹2,000 and above per sq ft

Imported stone, bespoke joinery, smart home automation, luxury brand fittings — Kohler, Grohe, Duravit. For villa projects and high-end residences where material quality and design originality are the primary brief.

Room-Wise Interior Design Cost in Dehradun

Per sq ft rates are a starting point — but interior costs vary significantly by room. Some spaces cost far more per sq ft than others because of the work involved.

Kitchen — ₹1.5 lakh to ₹6 lakh

The most expensive room per sq ft in any home. Modular kitchen cost depends on the number of shutters, countertop material — granite, quartz, or Corian — appliance cutouts, and chimney/hob provisions. In Dehradun, a well-done mid-range modular kitchen for a standard 3 BHK typically costs between ₹2 lakh and ₹3.5 lakh.

Master Bedroom — ₹80,000 to ₹2.5 lakh

Includes wardrobe, false ceiling, flooring, lighting, and TV unit if required. Premium wardrobes with veneer finish or lacquered shutters are the biggest cost driver here.

Living and Dining — ₹1 lakh to ₹4 lakh

False ceiling design, flooring — marble, vitrified, or wooden — TV unit, and furniture. This is the room most homeowners invest in most heavily because it sets the visual tone of the entire home.

Bathrooms — ₹60,000 to ₹2 lakh each

Tile selection, sanitary fittings brand, and shower enclosure are the main variables. Premium fittings from brands like Jaquar or Kohler can double bathroom cost compared to standard options.

What is Typically Not Included in Interior Design Quotes in Dehradun

This is where most homeowners get surprised mid-project. A standard interior design quote in Dehradun may not include:

  • Civil work — tile fixing, plastering, waterproofing
  • Electrical point additions or rewiring
  • False ceiling civil structure — only the design, not execution
  • Curtains and soft furnishings
  • Loose furniture — sofa, dining table, beds
  • AC installation and ducting

Always ask your designer for a scope-inclusive quote — a number without a clear scope is not a quote, it is an opening bid.

Interior Design Fees in Dehradun — How Designers Charge

Beyond the material and execution costs, the designer's professional fee is a separate line item. In Dehradun, interior designers typically charge in one of three ways:

Per Sq Ft Fee

The most common model for full-home projects. Designers in Dehradun typically charge between ₹40 and ₹150 per sq ft as a professional design fee, depending on their experience and the scope of services. This covers concept design, drawings, material selection, and site supervision.

Percentage of Project Cost

Some designers charge 7% to 15% of the total project value as their fee. This model works well for larger projects where the scope is clearly defined from the beginning.

Fixed Package

For specific rooms or defined scopes — a 2 BHK package or a kitchen-plus-two-bedroom package — a fixed fee is agreed upfront. Useful for clients who want full cost clarity before committing.

What Makes Interior Design in Dehradun Different

Dehradun has specific conditions that affect interior design choices and costs in ways that a Delhi or Pune designer may not immediately understand.

Moisture and climate — Dehradun receives significant rainfall and has higher humidity than many plains cities. Material choices that ignore this — cheap MDF, low-grade laminates, non-waterproof plywood in wet areas — deteriorate faster here. Good interior design in Dehradun accounts for the local climate in every material selection.

Mountain light — The quality of natural light in Dehradun is different from plains cities. Homes here often have better natural light orientation, and good interior design amplifies this rather than fighting it with artificial lighting.

Local material availability — Not all materials available in Delhi are readily available in Dehradun. A designer rooted here knows what is stocked locally, what needs to be imported, and how lead times affect project timelines.
