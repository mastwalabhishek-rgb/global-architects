Looking for an Architect of Record in India? Partner with Global Architects

June 2026 / 6 min read / Global Architects

International architecture and design firms are increasingly being invited to design homes, resorts, workplaces and mixed-use projects in India. The opportunity is strong, but the barriers are real. Local building codes, municipal approvals, state byelaws, site conditions, contractor coordination and day-to-day execution cannot be solved by design talent alone.

A concept can be exceptional and still struggle on ground if the local process is not handled carefully. Drawings need to respond to the National Building Code, state development controls, fire and safety requirements, services coordination, structural inputs, contractor capability and the realities of Indian construction sites. This is where a trusted local partner becomes essential.

What the Architect of Record Model Means

The Architect of Record, or Local Associate Architect, model allows an international design firm to retain creative control while a locally registered and experienced architecture practice handles compliance, approvals and execution support in India. The design intent remains with the lead design firm. The local partner translates that intent into buildable, compliant documentation and supports the project through the approval and construction process.

In a typical collaboration, the international firm develops the concept, design language, planning direction and client-facing design package. The local associate reviews the work against Indian code requirements, coordinates with structural and services consultants where needed, prepares local submission drawings, assists with municipal approvals and stays involved during site execution so the project does not drift away from the approved design.

For international studios, this model protects both design quality and delivery confidence. It reduces avoidable redesign, prevents approval delays, and creates a local point of accountability for decisions that must be made quickly on site.

Global Architects as Your Local Execution Partner

Global Architects is based in Dehradun, Uttarakhand and is led by Principal Architect Ar. Shailesh Kumar. The studio works across architecture, interiors, visualization and construction coordination, with a process shaped by real site experience as much as design thinking.

For international firms entering India, our role is practical and precise: review the design for NBC and state byelaw compliance, coordinate local documentation, assist with approvals, supervise site progress, communicate with contractors and help maintain the integrity of the original design through execution. Our architecture services are especially useful when the project needs both design sensitivity and disciplined local follow-through.

Our location gives us a strong working understanding of projects in Dehradun and across Uttarakhand, where terrain, climate, seismic requirements and local authority processes often demand more attention than a generic India-wide approach can provide. For hill and resort destinations in particular, local judgement affects everything from access planning to drainage, retaining structures, material movement and contractor sequencing.

Project Scope for Collaboration

Global Architects is open to Architect of Record and Local Associate Architect collaborations across residential, hospitality and commercial projects. This includes premium homes, villas, resort developments, boutique hotels, offices, showrooms and mixed-use spaces where an international design vision needs an Indian execution partner with local code and site experience.

Uttarakhand offers a strong opportunity for international firms working on hill residences, wellness retreats, resort properties and boutique hospitality projects. At the same time, these projects need serious attention to slope stability, approvals, road access, rainwater management, seismic design and construction supervision. A local team that understands these conditions can help the design travel from presentation to site with far less friction.

How the Partnership Works in Practice

The best partnerships are clear from the start. The lead design firm defines the creative brief, client expectations and design standards. Global Architects supports the project with local compliance review, authority documentation, consultant coordination, site observations and contractor communication. We can work discreetly behind the lead studio or directly with the client where the project structure requires it.

The goal is not to dilute the design. The goal is to protect it. Local execution should help the original idea survive approvals, budgets, procurement, site decisions and construction realities.
