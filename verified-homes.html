Our review

How We Verify a Home

01

Documents review

We review the documents made available for the home, including registry, mutation, map and tax receipts, to organise the information a buyer may wish to examine.

02

Structural visual inspection

Our team makes a visual architectural observation of the accessible built condition, noting elements that deserve attention in a future discussion.

03

Area and measurement check

We check available area information against on-site measurements where practical, giving the listing a clearer starting point.

04

Floor plan by Global Architects

Each selected home receives a clear floor plan by Global Architects, helping you understand the layout before a site visit.

Verification is a documentation and visual review, not a legal title guarantee. Buyers should still take independent legal advice and complete their own due diligence before making a decision.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does verified mean?

Verified means Global Architects has completed a documentation and visual review of the home. It is not a legal title guarantee, and buyers should take independent legal advice before proceeding.

What documents are reviewed?

We review the registry, mutation record, site map and property tax receipts, along with a visual check of the structure and a measurement check of the home. This is a documentation and visual review, not a legal title guarantee.

How do I list my home?

Message us on WhatsApp to discuss your home and whether it may be suitable for Verified Homes.

Who is Verified Homes for?

Verified Homes is for buyers who want a home presented with clear documents and an architect's honest notes, and for owners in Dehradun who want to discuss listing their home with Global Architects. Message us on WhatsApp to get started.

Can you help renovate after purchase?

Yes. Global Architects can discuss renovation, interior design and planning for a home after purchase.
