The Founder

Ar. Shailesh Kumar

Principal Architect & Founder

Born and raised in Uttarakhand, Ar. Shailesh Kumar grew up surrounded by some of the most powerful natural architecture on Earth — the Himalayas. It gave him an instinct for scale, proportion, and the relationship between built form and landscape that no school can teach.

After graduating with honours in Architecture and completing his registration with the Council of Architecture, he worked with leading firms in Delhi and Mumbai before returning to Dehradun in 2017 with a clear mission: to build an architecture practice that was rooted in this landscape, but capable of delivering world-class design anywhere in India.

In eight years, Global Architects has completed over 500 projects across 10+ cities — from intimate hill residences to large-scale commercial developments — without once compromising on the belief that architecture must serve the people who live inside it.

Our Philosophy

Six Principles That
Guide Every Decision

01 — Place

Architecture is geography made visible

Every building we design begins with the land it will stand on. The slope, the sun path, the prevailing breeze, the view — these are not constraints, they are the design.

02 — People

Buildings are for living, not looking

We spend hours understanding how our clients live before we draw a single line. Architecture that does not serve its inhabitants is sculpture, not architecture.

03 — Light

Light is the first material

Before concrete, before glass, before timber — we design with light. How does morning enter? Where does afternoon pool? What does dusk do to this wall?

04 — Honesty

Materials deserve to be themselves

We do not paint concrete to look like marble or cover steel in cladding. Every material we use is expressed honestly — its texture, its grain, its age, its truth.

05 — Craft

Details are not decoration — they are design

The junction between a concrete wall and a timber floor. The shadow gap beneath a cabinet. These are the moments where architecture becomes craft, and craft becomes art.

06 — Time

Great buildings age beautifully

We choose materials and forms that improve with time — that develop patina, character, and meaning as decades pass. Architecture should outlive its architects.

"We have never built a building we are not proud of. We never will."

How We Work

Our Process in Full

01

Discovery &
Listening

The first meeting is never in the office. We visit the site, walk the land, sit with the family at the time of day they love most. We ask about the morning light, about the tree they cannot remove, about the view from the kitchen window they have always dreamed of. Only when we understand how you want to live do we begin to think about architecture.

4–8 weeks

02

Concept Design

Hand sketches first — always. We present three distinct concepts, each a different interpretation of the brief. No renders yet. Drawings and models only — so the conversation stays about ideas, not finishes.

6–10 weeks

03

Design Development

The chosen concept is developed in full detail. Materials are selected, structural system resolved, MEP integrated. 3D models and detailed drawings presented. Every detail drawn by hand before it is built.

8–14 weeks

04

Approvals & Tendering

We handle all statutory approvals — MDDA, municipal, structural NOC. We prepare tender documents and help select a contractor. We believe the right contractor is as important as the right design.

8–16 weeks

05

Construction

We are on site. Every week, without exception. We review work, resolve issues, make decisions. Architecture is made during construction — in the gap between drawing and reality, that is where craft happens.

12–24 months

06

Handover & Beyond

We visit every completed project six months and one year after handover. We want to know: does the light do what we intended? Are you living the way you dreamed? This is not business — it is accountability.
