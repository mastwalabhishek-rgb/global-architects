Architect Fees in Dehradun: What It Costs to Hire an Architect in 2026

Last updated: October 2026 | By Global Architects, Dehradun

Short answer: In Dehradun, architect fees for a residential project typically run between ₹50 and ₹150 per sq ft for standard design services, and ₹150 to ₹400+ per sq ft for premium or complex projects. Percentage-based quotes usually fall between 5% and 10% of construction cost. For a 2,000 sq ft house, that works out to roughly ₹1 lakh (concept-level scope) to ₹3.5 lakh (full design, drawings and coordination).

These are indicative market ranges, last reviewed in October 2026. The final fee depends on scope, site and design complexity.

How Do Architects Charge in Dehradun?

Architects here use four common fee models. Each suits a different kind of project.

Fee modelHow it worksTypical rangeBest suited for

Which model is "best" depends on how clear your scope is. A fixed or per sq ft fee works when the brief is clear. A percentage model works better when the design is expected to evolve.

What Is Included in an Architect's Fee — and What Usually Isn't

Typically included

  • Concept design and space planning
  • 3D views of the design
  • Drawings for MDDA map submission
  • Working drawings for construction
  • Coordination with the structural engineer
  • Site visits, as per the agreed scope

Usually charged separately

  • Structural design fee (at many firms)
  • Fees paid to MDDA or other authorities (paid by the client directly)
  • Interior design and landscaping
  • Survey and soil testing
  • Additional revisions after design sign-off

Always ask for the inclusions in writing. Two quotes can look similar and cover very different amounts of work.

Architect Fees vs. Interior Design Fees

These are different services with different pricing. Architectural fees cover the building: layout, structure coordination, elevations and drawings. Interior design fees cover finishes, furniture, lighting and woodwork inside it. Mixing the two is the most common reason quotes look confusing.

What Affects Architect Fees in Dehradun?

  • Built-up area and number of floors: larger projects take more drawings and coordination.
  • Design complexity: courtyards, jaali screens, louvres and custom elevations take more design time than a standard layout.
  • Plot conditions: sloped or hill-side plots need extra planning and levels work.
  • Earthquake-resistant detailing: Dehradun lies in a high seismic region, so structural coordination matters.
  • Scope of service: design-only versus design plus supervision.
  • Approvals support: MDDA submission and related drawings.
  • Experience of the practice: a firm with a long portfolio prices that experience in.

Where the Council of Architecture Scale Fits In

The Council of Architecture (COA) publishes a recommended scale of professional charges, usually expressed as a percentage of project cost linked to stages of service. Many architects use it as a reference point, and fees in practice vary by project type and scope.

Questions to Ask Before You Hire an Architect

  1. Is the architect registered with the Council of Architecture?
  2. What exactly is included: 3D views, working drawings, structural coordination, site visits?
  3. How is the fee paid: in stages tied to deliverables?
  4. How many design revisions are included?
  5. Can I see completed projects similar to mine in Dehradun?

Common Questions

Architect Fees in Dehradun FAQs

How much do architects charge in Dehradun?

For residential projects, architects in Dehradun typically charge ₹50–₹150 per sq ft for standard design services (₹150–₹400+ for premium or complex work), or about 5%–10% of construction cost. The final fee depends on scope and complexity.

Is the architect's fee a percentage or per sq ft?

Both are common. Some architects charge a percentage of construction cost, others a rate per sq ft of built-up area, and some a fixed package. The right model depends on how clearly the scope is defined.

What does an architect's fee usually include?

Usually concept design, 3D views, drawings for MDDA submission, working drawings, coordination with the structural engineer and site visits as agreed. Structural design fees, government fees, interiors and landscaping are often separate, so confirm in writing.

Why do architect quotes differ so much?

Quotes differ because scope, experience, design complexity and the number of site visits differ. A lower quote may cover less work, so compare inclusions, not just the number.

Can I hire an architect for design only and build with my own contractor?

Yes. Many clients take design and working drawings and build with their own contractor. Site supervision is usually an additional scope.
